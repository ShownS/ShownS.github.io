This portfolio organizes my work into three main areas: mechatronics and integrated prototyping, robotics and control simulation, and engineering practice and hands-on experience. These categories reflect my interest in mechanical systems that connect design, sensing, controls, software, and practical testing.

## Project Areas

### [Mechatronics and Integrated Prototyping](/projects/mechatronics/)

Mechanical design, sensing, electronics, and control brought together in working robotic systems.

### [Robotics and Control Simulation](/projects/controls-simulation/)

Simulation-based work focused on robotic motion, control behavior, and system modeling.

### [Engineering Practice and Hands-On Experience](/projects/engineering-practice/)

Professional, instructional, and independent work that strengthened my practical engineering judgment, communication skills, and hands-on mechanical experience.
