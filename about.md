I am a mechanical engineering graduate student at Oregon State University. My background is primarily in mechanical design and mechatronics, with a focus on system integration. My experiences so far have included design, integration, simulation, prototyping, and working with multidisciplinary teams. I am especially interested in the development and testing of mechatronic systems that combine sensors, controls, and software integration.

My engineering experience has developed through both academic and hands-on work. Through my minor in robotics, I worked on projects using mechanical design, wiring, programming, testing, and iteration. In simulation projects, I explored how learning-based and classical controllers can be used to study robotic systems. I also value my hands-on restoration and modification work, as it strengthened my understanding of how mechanical systems interact and showed the importance of careful testing and problem solving.

I've come to value communication as an important part of my professional development. Working on teaching teams for undergraduate engineering classes gave me the opportunity to practice explaining technical details to an audience with a wide variety of experience levels. Working with multidisciplinary teams further reinforced this, showing me the importance of asking questions to learn from those with different backgrounds.

My goal is to contribute to engineering teams in product design and mechatronics, combining mechanical design, prototyping, integration, and communication to develop and test useful systems.
