I am a mechanical engineering graduate student with a background in mechanical product development, mechatronics, and robotics. My experience includes mechanical design, system integration, simulation, prototyping, and multidisciplinary communication. I am especially interested in developing and testing mechanical systems that include sensors, controls, or software integration.

My engineering experience has developed through a mix of academic and hands-on work. Through my robotics minor, I have worked on projects involving mechanical design, wiring, programming, validation, and iteration. In simulation-based projects, I have explored how learning-based and classical controllers can be used to study robotic systems. I also value hands-on restoration and modification work because it strengthened my understanding of how mechanical components interact and reinforced the importance of careful testing and problem solving.

Communication has also become an important part of my development. Working on teaching teams for undergraduate classes allowed me to practice explaining technical details clearly and adapting my communication for different levels of experience. My work with multidisciplinary teams has also shown me the importance of asking questions and learning from people with different technical backgrounds.

My goal is to contribute to engineering teams in product design and mechatronics, where I can combine mechanical design, prototyping, system integration, and communication to help develop and test useful systems.
